# Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboards

A collection of two multi-page Power BI dashboards covering end-to-end business analytics 
across Marketing, Customer, Sales, and Supply Chain domains.

## Dashboard 1 — Marketing & Customer Analytics

### Pages
| Page | Focus |
|------|-------|
| Campaign ROI | Evaluates marketing spend efficiency across channels and campaign types |
| Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) | Segments customers by revenue contribution, AOV, and loyalty tier |
| Customer Retention | Tracks churn rate, cart abandonment, satisfaction scores, and login activity |

### Key Metrics Tracked
- Total Campaign Spend & Revenue, Campaign ROI, Avg Conversion Rate
- Revenue per Customer, Average Order Value (AOV), CLV by segment
- Churn Rate, Cart Abandon Rate, Customer Satisfaction Score

### Visuals Used
Clustered column/bar charts, area charts, donut charts, stacked area charts, 
KPI cards, pivot tables, slicers

---

## Dashboard 2 — Sales, Inventory & Product Analytics

### Pages
| Page | Focus |
|------|-------|
| Geographic Sales | Regional breakdown of sales volume, delivery time, and shipping costs |
| Inventory Efficiency | Tracks turnover ratios, backorder rates, stock utilization, and supplier metrics |
| Product Performance | Compares revenue, cost, and profit across product categories |
| Return Analysis | Analyses return rates and associated handling/storage costs |

### Key Metrics Tracked
- Total Sales, Revenue, Profit, Avg Order Value
- Inventory Turnover, Backorder Rate, Stock Utilization, Avg Lead Time
- Average Return Rate, Handling Cost, Storage Cost, Supplier Rating

### Visuals Used
Line charts, scatter charts, 100% stacked columns, pie/donut charts,
clustered bar/column charts, KPI cards, slicers

---

## Tools & Skills Demonstrated
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data modelling:** Star schema with date tables per entity
- **DAX measures:** Custom KPIs for ROI, churn, turnover, and profitability
- **UX:** Storm theme, rounded card borders, cross-visual drill filtering
- **Domains covered:** Marketing analytics, CRM, supply chain, product analytics
